Joseph Benjamin R. Ilagan

Assistant Professor, John Gokongwei School of Management, Ateneo de Manila University Director, Ateneo Business Insights Laboratory for Development (BUILD) Program Director, BS Information Technology Entrepreneurship

jbilagan@ateneo.edu Google Scholar ORCID LinkedIn

I build and evaluate AI systems for learning and for small businesses, using design-based research. Current work covers conversational tutoring and learning simulations for entrepreneurship and management education, AI-assisted workflows in research and administration, data tools for micro and small enterprises, and digital public infrastructure for identity verification. I joined Ateneo full-time in 2019 after more than 26 years in industry and completed a PhD in Computer Science in 2026.
