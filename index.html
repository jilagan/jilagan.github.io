Conversational tutoring and learning simulations

Tutors and simulated characters built on large language models for entrepreneurship and management students. Students interview simulated customers and test their financial models with a simulated mentor. Teachers can write these simulations in plain language. This was the subject of my PhD dissertation, The Design and Use of Conversational Intelligent Tutoring Systems and Customer Validation Simulation in Technology Entrepreneurship Education (2026), supervised by Ma. Mercedes T. Rodrigo.

Projects

LiteChat

A platform for running classroom simulations on several large language models. VentureBot, the customer-interview simulation in my dissertation, runs on it. Funded by an Ateneo URC grant, Simulation-Enabled Lessons in Higher Education.

Reading small-business paper records

Research on reading the handwritten logbooks and receipts of small Philippine businesses with OCR and large language models, and turning them into sales, inventory and receivables records. This work led to KLARO.

  • Applied Optical Character Recognition and Large Language Models in Augmenting Manual Business Processes for Data Analytics in Traditional Small Businesses with Minimal Digital Adoption Artificial Intelligence in HCI (HCII 2025), 2025
  • AI-Powered Enterprise System for CRM and Inventory Management in Low-Digital MSMEs HCI in Business, Government and Organizations (HCII 2026), 2026
  • Can a low-cost app read a food stall's notebook? (HCII 2025) Video

Ateneo Technical Resource Lab (TRL)

Run by BUILD and launched with Co-Develop in May 2024. It runs the DPI Experience Center and built the eKYC extension for National ID verification, which DICT and PSA approved in November 2025.

Breathe Metro Manila

An air quality coalition of Manila Observatory, the Ateneo School of Medicine and Public Health, Clarity Movement and BUILD, which publishes real-time air quality readings for Metro Manila. I lead BUILD's part. BUILD built the coalition's iPhone and Apple Watch apps for reading the data.

Tom Taps

A picture board for children who don't speak, or don't speak yet. They tap pictures to build sentences that the app says aloud in English, Filipino or Spanish. Built with speech-language pathologists and published by Archmob Pte Ltd for iPhone and iPad.

Kanji Mentor

A kanji writing app for JLPT learners. It hides each kanji until you draw it from memory, then reveals it and uses AI to check your handwriting for balance, stroke order and spacing. Reviews follow a spaced repetition schedule. Published by Archmob Pte Ltd for iPhone and iPad.

Katipunan traffic explainer

A ten-minute video on why traffic on Katipunan Avenue keeps getting worse, made with an AI-assisted pipeline. Each version is posted, and viewer comments go into the next one. It is on version 4.
